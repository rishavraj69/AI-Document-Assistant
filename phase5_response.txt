How the RAG prompt works
The prompt is built in backend/app/services/gemini_service.py and is structured as:

CONTEXT:
[retrieved chunks]

QUESTION:
[user question]

The prompt explicitly instructs Gemini to:
- answer using ONLY the provided document context,
- not invent information,
- say when the information is not available in the document,
- stay concise and useful.

How retrieved chunks are passed to Gemini
The /test-rag endpoint:
1. extracts and chunks the PDF,
2. builds the FAISS index,
3. retrieves the top 3 chunks,
4. sends those chunks to GeminiService.generate_answer(...)

That service turns the retrieved chunks into a grounded prompt and calls the Gemini SDK.
